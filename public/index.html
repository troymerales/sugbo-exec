Lead generation funnel

Lead cohort = prospects whose first engagement falls in the reporting period, followed to the end of the data. Sources: cold calling, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, website (organic/direct) and referral.

Lead funnel: engage → inquire → demo → free trial → convert → re-subscribe
